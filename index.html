Frequently asked questions

Privacy, accuracy and the limits of statistical detection.

Is my text uploaded anywhere?

No. Model weights and the language model ship with the page. All scoring happens in your browser. Your text never touches a server, and recent analyses are kept only in this tab's session storage.

How accurate is it?

The classifier reaches 99.4% accuracy on its training-distribution test split — roughly 900 labeled samples drawn from a Brown corpus human baseline and GPT-3.5/4 output. That split does not represent the full range of modern AI writing styles, short passages, or paraphrased text. Real-world accuracy is meaningfully lower. Treat the score as one signal among many, not a definitive verdict.

How much text do I need?

Three or more full sentences is the practical minimum, and 150+ words gives steadier results. Very short inputs don't contain enough variation for the statistical signals to separate cleanly.
